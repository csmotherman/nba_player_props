Precision shows how many pregame-eligible players did not play (injured/rested): that is the missing-availability problem an external injury feed would address. Training rows are currently the players who actually played, which conditions on the outcome of availability; this selection effect must be handled in any modelling.

## 12. Known gaps and risks

* The stats API throttled the collector after roughly 900 requests at ~1.5 req/s (hung connections; the host answered its homepage but not `/stats/`). Pacing was lowered to 1.5 s with one worker and a circuit breaker was added. Collection is resumable; what was and was not collected is stated in sections 2 and 6.
* Regular season only; no playoffs/play-in/preseason.
* Official inactives are not a complete historical record (section 6).
* 2026-27 schedule listing is incomplete (section 4).
* Completion time is an assumption (`COMPLETION_BUFFER_H`).
* No injury/status feed, so pregame availability for the target game is unknown beyond lagged appearances.
* No forecast accuracy has been evaluated; all `forecast_accuracy` verification levels are `untested`.
